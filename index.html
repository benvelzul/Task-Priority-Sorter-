Assignment Priority Sorter

Your personal study command centre
How it works
📋
Add assignments with a due date, difficulty (0–10), estimated time needed, and how much you enjoy it.
🏆
Priority score is calculated from urgency (due date), difficulty, time remaining, and enjoyment. Enjoyment gives a small bonus — unpleasant tasks rank higher.
☑️
Checklists let you break any assignment into smaller steps. Tick them off as you go.
✏️
Edit or delete any assignment at any time using the icons on each card.
🍅 Pomodoro timer
⏱️
25 min focus → 5 min short break → repeat 4 times → 15 min long break. Based on the Pomodoro Technique by Francesco Cirillo.
📊
Time studied is automatically logged to your assignment, reducing its "time left" and pushing it down the priority list as you make progress.
💾 Your data
🔒
All data is saved locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. Clearing your browser data will erase assignments.
